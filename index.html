Type plain English and HoloVox turns it into phonemes, then sings them with formant synthesis — a sawtooth glottal source shaped by three tuned resonators that glide through every syllable: consonants, vowel, endings and all. 100% Web Audio, zero samples, zero servers. Your words never leave the browser.

An original homage to singing synthesizers like Vocaloid. Not affiliated with Yamaha or Crypton Future Media.
